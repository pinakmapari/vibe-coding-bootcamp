# Prototype: Loop

Source: Juan's prototype repo README, roadmap and live app (checked 2026-11-24). Live app: https://reusablehousholdlisting.lovable.app. Code is in Juan's private repo and stays there, not in this wiki repo.

## What it is (evidence: README and live app)
A mobile-friendly student household exchange. A student trades in kitchenware or small appliances, accepts an inspected offer in Loop Coins, then spends the coins on Loop-held inventory. No direct swaps between students.

## Built (per README; not independently tested by us)
- Browse 12 sample products with search, filters, sorting and item pages
- Trade-in with photos, indicative estimate and simulated drop-off
- Accept or decline an inspected offer; wallet, checkout reservations, orders, cancellations
- Accounts, and an admin workspace for inspections, inventory, orders and support
- Guided demo on the landing page: trade in a pan for 10 coins, then spend them on a 12-coin rice cooker

## Boundaries stated in the README
Payments and appointments are simulated. Sample inventory and photos. Coins are purchasing credits only (illustrative: 1 coin = £1, no fee). No claim of proven demand, safety certification or environmental savings.

## Assumptions (not evidenced yet)
- Students who move out would trade items in for credits rather than bin or give them away.
- Students would buy second-hand kitchenware from a held inventory.

## Risks against the judging criteria
- Criterion 4 asks for one main task that works. This build has accounts, admin, wallet and checkout. The pitch demo should show one path only (the guided demo trade-in then spend).
- Criterion 3 needs the prototype to answer the problem card. Check the card's "what they do now" against a trade-in-for-credit model.
- Criterion 5 needs a test with another team. No test notes exist yet.
