# Science for Humanity areas and starter problems

Source: handbook p23 to p29. Judging includes how clearly the project connects to Science for Humanity. Teams may write their own problem.

| Area | Starter problems |
|---|---|
| Climate, energy and sustainability | Students forget what food they have and throw it away; Students throw away usable belongings when they move out; People are unsure which bin to put their rubbish in |
| Human and artificial intelligence | Students get AI explanations that are too complicated; Students miss deadlines buried in long university emails; Students get answers from AI without learning how to solve the problem |
| Health, medtech and robotics | People forget questions they wanted to ask their doctor; Students sit for hours without taking a break; Families struggle to organise practical help for an elderly relative |
| Space, security science and telecommunications | Student societies lose track of who manages their online accounts; Students are unsure whether a rental advert is a scam; Students struggle to find somewhere with reliable Wi-Fi for an online class |

Choosing an area: ask who has the problem and what would get better for them if it were solved. The same tool can serve different areas depending on the question asked.
