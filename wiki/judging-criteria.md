# Judging criteria and pitch

Source: handbook p48 to p52. Each criterion scored 1 to 5, total out of 25. Commercial potential and presentation polish are not scored.

| # | Criterion | Judges look for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem and evidence | A specific person with a specific problem, backed by what people wrote on Reddit |
| 2 | Science for Humanity connection | A clear link to the strategy, and who benefits if the problem is solved |
| 3 | Solution fit | A product that answers the problem on the problem card |
| 4 | Working prototype | A published prototype that can be opened and used for its main task |
| 5 | Learning and working with AI | What changed after testing, what was learned, and whether to continue, change direction or stop, with reasons |

Stopping the project, with reasons, is a strong answer on criterion 5.

## Pitch: 3 minutes (firm limit), then 2 minutes of questions
- Problem, 45s: who, what the research revealed, link to Science for Humanity
- Solution, 20s: what we built and how it could help
- Demo, 90s: one complete task in the prototype
- What's next, 25s: what we learned; continue, change direction or stop

Have a backup screenshot in case the link doesn't load. Format: every team pitches in one of two rooms; each room picks a finalist; the two finalists pitch again and one team wins.
