# Handbook: what it requires

Source: "Vibe Coding Bootcamp handbook" (Imperial Enterprise Lab with Lovable), stored in the team Drive folder.

## Principles
1. Fall in love with the problem, not the first solution.
2. Get the solution into users' hands quickly.
3. When you don't know, ask AI (use Chat or Plan mode in Lovable, which don't change the app).
4. Make your thinking visible.

## Day 1
- **Choose a problem** (one of 12 starter problems, or our own) and note its Science for Humanity area. A quick choice is fine; it can be refined after research.
- **WIP website**: brief = team name, who and problem, team introduction, look and feel. Lovable starter prompt builds a mobile-friendly site with an email sign-up only. It must say the solution is in development and must not invent testimonials, results, features or a launch date. Publish and test the sign-up with a real email.
- **Research**: find 2 to 3 Reddit threads where people describe the problem (search `site:reddit.com` plus keywords). Use the handbook's problem card prompt in Lovable **Chat mode**. Card headings: Problem title, Who and when, What is difficult, What happens as a result, What do they do now, Supporting quote, Source. Check each card against its thread; the quote must be word for word; missing info is "Not stated". Keep one card.

## Day 2
Explore templates, choose an approach, write a product overview, plan and build the prototype, publish, have another team test it, improve it, update the WIP site.

## Lovable working method
Plan a small chunk, build it, check it, revert if it goes wrong. One change at a time; use Plan mode for anything bigger than a tweak; press "Try to fix" once or twice, then switch to Plan mode with the error or a screenshot and ask for the root cause without changes. Prompt shape: Where + What + Don't touch. Credits: Chat is cheap, Plan about 1 credit per message, Build roughly 0.5 to 2.
